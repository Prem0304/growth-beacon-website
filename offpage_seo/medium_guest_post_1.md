## The Shift to Intent-Driven Digital Search

Over 82% of consumers in Tamil Nadu search on Google before making a purchasing decision for high-value retail items, commercial services, or local consultations. 1. **Google Business Profile & Local Maps Optimization**:

2. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**:

3. **High-ROI Meta & Google Search Ads**:
