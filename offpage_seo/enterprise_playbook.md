# Enterprise & MNC High-DA Backlink Acquisition Playbook — Growth Beacon

## Strategy 1: Institutional & Educational (.edu / .ac.in) Sponsorship Backlinks
- **Objective**: Acquire high-authority DA 75–90 `.ac.in` / `.edu.in` backlinks from engineering colleges and management institutes in Tamil Nadu (e.g. Anna University affiliates, PSG Tech Coimbatore, Thiagarajar College Madurai).
- **Tactics**:
  1. **Digital Marketing Workshop Sponsorship**: Sponsor annual tech symposiums or entrepreneurship summits with a ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 sponsorship in exchange for a official sponsor backlink on the `.ac.in` event page.
  2. **Student Internship Portal Listing**: Partner with college placement cells to list Growth Beacon's digital marketing internship program.

## Strategy 2: Government & Regional Commerce Association Listings (.gov.in / Chamber of Commerce)
- **Objective**: Secure DA 80+ citations from Indian business federations.
- **Directories to Target**:
  - **Tamil Nadu Chamber of Commerce & Industry (TNCCI)**
  - **Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Southern Region**
  - **MADITSSIA (Madurai District Tiny & Small Scale Industries Association)**
  - **CODISSIA (Coimbatore District Small Industries Association)**

## Strategy 3: Digital Marketing Case Study Syndication
- **Objective**: Syndicate performance marketing case studies to B2B platforms (YourStory, Inc42, Business Standard).
- **Key Metric Highlighted**: "How a Bodinayakanur agency helped a regional showroom scale leads by 180% using Google Maps Local SEO & Meta Ads."
