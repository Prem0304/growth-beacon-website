1. What WhatsApp Marketing Does & Who Needs It

WhatsApp Marketing & API Automation is conversational marketing that allows businesses to interact instantly with prospective buyers inside WhatsApp. By combining Click-to-WhatsApp Meta Ads with automated chatbot flows, businesses can qualify leads, answer FAQs, send product catalogs, and collect customer feedback 24/7. It is essential for retail showrooms, real estate developers, educational academies, clinics, and local services in South Tamil Nadu.

2. Business Problems It Solves

  • High Lead Drop-Off: Potential buyers filling forms but failing to answer phone calls later in the day.
  • Slow Lead Response Times: Prospects waiting hours for replies, by which time they have contacted a local competitor.
  • Low Email Open Rates: Traditional email marketing getting ignored, whereas WhatsApp achieves 95%+ open rates.
  • Manual Representative Messaging: Sales reps spending hours copy-pasting repetitive product details to individual leads.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between WhatsApp Business App and WhatsApp API?
WhatsApp Business App is limited to single-device manual use, whereas WhatsApp API allows multi-agent logins, automated webhooks, custom chatbots, and bulk broadcast messaging.
Are messages sent via WhatsApp API compliant with WhatsApp policy?
Yes! We use official Meta WhatsApp Business API channels with pre-approved message templates ensuring 100% policy compliance.
