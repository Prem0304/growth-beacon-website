Turn social media attention into measurable business revenue. We build performance Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), high-converting video reel creatives, custom audience targeting, and Click-to-WhatsApp ad funnels for businesses across Theni, Madurai, Dindigul, and Coimbatore.

1. What Meta Advertising Does & Who Needs It

Direct Answer: Meta Advertising (Facebook & Instagram Ads) displays visual image and video reel ads to targeted demographic audiences based on interests, geographic radius, age, and online behavior. It is designed for businesses looking to generate immediate lead inquiries, drive retail showroom footfall, or build brand awareness across South Tamil Nadu.

2. Business Problems It Solves

  • Wasted Ad Budgets: Running boosted posts without proper Meta Pixel tracking, custom conversion events, or audience exclusion rules.
  • Ad Fatigue & Rising Cost-per-Lead (CPL): Creative ads burning out because no new ad formats or video reels are tested.
  • Unqualified Lead Inquiries: Receiving inquiries from users outside your target service area or purchasing capacity.
  • Lack of Retargeting: Failing to re-engage prospective buyers who visited your website or clicked an ad without inquiring.

3. Services & Deliverables Included

  • Custom Audience Strategy & Setup: Lookalike audiences, interest targeting, and geographic pin-point radius targeting across Tamil Nadu cities.
  • High-Converting Video Reel & Graphic Ad Creatives: Vertical video reels, carousel ads, and promotional graphic banners.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp Ad Integration: Ads designed to launch pre-filled WhatsApp conversations instantly upon user click.
  • Meta Pixel & Conversions API Setup: Server-side tracking ensuring accurate conversion data despite browser privacy restrictions.
  • A/B Split Testing & Campaign Scaling: Testing hooks, headlines, and call-to-action buttons to lower cost per lead.

Step 1: Funnel Architecture & Audience Mapping

What is the difference between boosting a post and running Meta Ads Manager campaigns?
Boosting a post simply increases vanity post likes. Meta Ads Manager allows advanced audience targeting, custom lead forms, server-side Pixel conversion tracking, retargeting funnels, and A/B split testing.
Do you handle ad creative design and copywriting?
Yes! We produce all graphic banners, vertical video reels, ad copy scripts, and landing page designs in-house.
